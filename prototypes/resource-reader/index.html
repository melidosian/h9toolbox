TD-H9 resource service (S1/S2)

Reads the radio's 16 MiB resource flash through the S1 or S2 service firmware. Sections 1–3 only send the stock handshake, read (R), and exit (E) commands. Only section 4 writes, and only on an S2 radio.

A radio running stock or another firmware fails the service check harmlessly. Disconnect other radio apps first and keep the radio close: a full chip read is about 524,000 Bluetooth round trips.

4. S2 sector write test

Needs the S2 image (service version 2) and two matching dumps of the region first. The test only runs on a fully erased voice, font, or resource sector. It programs a test pattern, reads it back, and erases the sector to 0xFF again. The radio refuses every other partition.

5. Boot logo (S2)

The startup logo is a raw 160 × 128 RGB565 (big-endian) image in the first 10 sectors of resource. Read it first. Writing rewrites only the sectors that differ: each is erased only if the radio still holds exactly what was read, every 32-byte block is verified by the radio, and the whole logo is read back at the end. Restore by choosing your resource backup .bin or a downloaded logo.
