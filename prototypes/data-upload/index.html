TD-H9 data upload research build only

Writes a game-data pack (for example the Freedoom E1M1 pack for the Doom firmware) to the radio's 16 MiB resource flash over Bluetooth. Needs H9 Toolbox Firmware. Only the erased tails of resource, font and voice are used; stock never writes or reads there. Restore erases exactly what was written. Rules: firmware-research/doom-firmware/README.md.

Disconnected

Upload a pack

Idle.

Restore

Erases the uploaded pack and its directory back to 0xFF, as the chip was before. Deep restore checks every sector of all three tails (about 4 MiB to read: slow).
