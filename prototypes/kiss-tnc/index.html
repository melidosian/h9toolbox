Browser KISS TNC

Packet monitor and AX.25 connection test. Connect a KISS-compatible TNC, or try simulated APRS packets in demo mode. Bluetooth and serial connections stay in your browser.

TD-H9 packet mode uses the AF00 service, AF01 write and AF02 notifications. The radio needs H9 Toolbox KISS firmware and its APRS modem update. Tune to an authorized packet gateway before connecting.

Connection

BLE KISS endpoint

Enter UUIDs supplied by your TNC. TX is the characteristic the browser writes; RX is the characteristic it subscribes to.

Serial endpoint

Select an existing KISS serial port. For Bluetooth Classic SPP, pair in your operating system first; browser support for paired Bluetooth ports varies. The TNC must already be in KISS mode.
