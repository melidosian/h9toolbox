Browser KISS TNC

APRS packet transport proof of concept. Connect a KISS-compatible TNC, or try simulated packets in demo mode. Bluetooth and serial connections stay in your browser.

The H9’s Bluetooth KISS endpoint is not yet verified. Its known FF00 programming service is not a confirmed packet interface. This page sends no programming handshake or automatic radio commands.

Connection

BLE KISS endpoint

Enter UUIDs supplied by your TNC. TX is the characteristic the browser writes; RX is the characteristic it subscribes to.

Serial endpoint

Select an existing KISS serial port. For Bluetooth Classic SPP, pair in your operating system first; browser support for paired Bluetooth ports varies. The TNC must already be in KISS mode.

Disconnected

Send an APRS packet

Enter a packet in monitor format. On hardware, use your own licensed callsign and the appropriate channel. This sends a packet to the TNC; it does not confirm RF transmission or delivery.

Packet monitor

RX and TX show AX.25 UI packets. Other KISS commands and undecodable frames remain visible as hex. Latest 200 entries; nothing is uploaded or saved.
