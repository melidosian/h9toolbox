# APRS sample output trace

Source: captured APRS v1.0.15 image, SHA-256 `1dbbc10425f186f22a4d82d73f139f1e3cc3388931b73eb8ed18b6e3087ccd33`, read in the `H9Aprs` Ghidra project.

At `0x080026D4`, stock initialization stores peripheral base `0x40012400` (literal at `0x080027A4`) into sample state at `0x20000560`. The sample callback at `0x08002EF8` calls `0x08002F82`, which reads base+`0x40`, the ADC data register at `0x40012440`. It stores the low 16 bits and passes a normalized signed sample to the demodulator `FUN_08002308`. Only afterward does it check transmit state and optionally request the next waveform sample from `FUN_08002468`. The output value is passed through `FUN_08005EF8`, which reverses the high four bits, and written into bits 4 through 7 of the GPIOA output-data register at `0x50000014`. The low four bits of that register are preserved. When no waveform is active, the high output nibble is `0x80`.

The stock vector table puts `FUN_08002234` in slot 28 (IRQ 12), and it calls `FUN_08002F8A`. That handler takes the sample path when ADC status and interrupt-enable bits 2 or 3 match, calls `FUN_08002EF8`, then writes `0xC` to the status register. `replacement-source/aprs/h9_aprs_controller.c` implements the normalization, demod-before-output order, nibble reversal, and inactive value; `h9_aprs_adc_board.*` and `h9_aprs_wave_output.*` bind register callbacks, and the diagnostic vector connects the sample IRQ branch.

The stock setup at `0x080022C0` enables the GPIOA clock at `0x40021034` and configures pins 4–7 as outputs with high speed and pull-up. `0x08003244` enables the ADC clock at `0x40021040` and configures PA1 for analog input. Both register sequences are in the replacement board modules. Stock `0x08002824` initializes timer `0x40012C00` through `0x080049C0`, `0x08003F20`, and `0x08003FD2`: RCC bit `0x800` at `0x40021040`, prescaler `0xB`, period `0xD1`, update event generation, trigger output selection `0x20`, and counter enable. This sequence is now in `h9_aprs_sample_timer.*`. At a nominal 24 MHz clock, the prescaler and period imply about 9.52 kHz, close to eight samples per 1200-baud APRS bit. That relation is an inference; neither the clock accuracy nor the ADC trigger path has been measured.

The ADC initializer at `0x080026D4` calls calibration/configuration helpers `0x08002CAC`, `0x080030A0`, `0x08002D6C`, and `0x080032C0`. `h9_aprs_adc_board.*` now pulses RCC ADC reset bit 20, enables its clock, starts calibration with a bounded wait for ADC control bit 31 to clear, applies the recovered CFGR1/CFGR2/sample-time values, selects channel 1, and enables conversion plus ADC IRQ 12 after status bit 0 indicates ready. The replacement uses a bounded ready check where stock uses a fixed delay. Host fake-register tests exercise success and timeout paths. The observed IRQ sample, watchdog, and end-of-sequence branches are ported; see [the IRQ trace](aprs-adc-irq.md). After the waveform write, stock increments the 32-bit sample counter pointed to by the literal at `0x08002F7C` (`0x200000E4` in this image). The replacement now counts each completed ADC callback in `H9AprsController.adc_sample_count`, including receive-only operation. The timer-to-ADC trigger, electrical signal path, sample rate, and decoding have not been validated on the radio. The linked APRS ELF is a diagnostic artifact, not a flashable firmware image.
