# APRS sample output trace

Source: captured APRS v1.0.15 image, SHA-256 `1dbbc10425f186f22a4d82d73f139f1e3cc3388931b73eb8ed18b6e3087ccd33`, read in the `H9Aprs` Ghidra project.

At `0x080026D4`, stock initialization stores peripheral base `0x40012400` (literal at `0x080027A4`) into sample state at `0x20000560`. The sample callback at `0x08002EF8` calls `0x08002F82`, which reads base+`0x40`, the ADC data register at `0x40012440`. It stores the low 16 bits and passes a normalized signed sample to the demodulator `FUN_08002308`. Only afterward does it check transmit state and optionally request the next waveform sample from `FUN_08002468`. The output value is passed through `FUN_08005EF8`, which reverses the high four bits, and written into bits 4 through 7 of the GPIOA output-data register at `0x50000014`. The low four bits of that register are preserved. When no waveform is active, the high output nibble is `0x80`.

The stock vector table puts `FUN_08002234` in slot 28 (IRQ 12), and it calls `FUN_08002F8A`. That handler takes the sample path when ADC status and interrupt-enable bits 2 or 3 match, calls `FUN_08002EF8`, then writes `0xC` to the status register. `replacement-source/aprs/h9_aprs_controller.c` implements the normalization, demod-before-output order, nibble reversal, and inactive value; `h9_aprs_adc_board.*` and `h9_aprs_wave_output.*` bind register callbacks, and the diagnostic vector now connects the sample IRQ branch. The replacement still lacks ADC initialization, GPIO pin-mode setup, IRQ enable, and the handler's other ADC conditions. The stock ADC initializer at `0x080026D4` calls calibration/configuration helpers `0x08002CAC`, `0x080030A0`, `0x08002D6C`, and `0x080032C0`; its sequence needs a bounded, source-backed port before the IRQ can run on the board. The electrical circuit and sample timing also require verification.
